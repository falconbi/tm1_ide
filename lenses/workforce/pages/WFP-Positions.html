Positions

Every Position in the chosen cost centre that month — what it is and who is in it. Pick a Position to act. People's own changes (pay, leavers, transfers, time away) are on the People page.

Source and state

HR — the Position came from the HR system. Planning — created in the plan (a TBH, "to be hired"); once HR opens the real Position, link the two on Match new Positions so the hire counts once. Filled, Planned hire (an approved hire has started), Vacant (open, costs nothing), Closed (doesn't exist that month).

Actions

Change… grade, budgeted FTE, location or the Position's salary (used for a vacant Position or a planned hire) from a month — the months before stay as they were. Move… to another cost centre from a month (same company). Move to company… — a Position never changes company: a new Position is made there, its people leave and are hired into it, and this one closes. Close… after a month (refused while anyone holds it or a hire is planned after it). Reopen… the same budget line within the same plan year. Cancel… a planning-created Position that is no longer needed.

Hire month is editable on an empty Position; Request a new position… creates a planning Position (TBH).

Who can change

HR and Finance (WFP HR / WFP Finance); everyone can read. Cost follows within about 5 minutes.
