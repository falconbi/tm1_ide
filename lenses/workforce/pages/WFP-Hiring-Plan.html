Hiring plan

Pick a hire line

Plan a hire month for a position that is empty in that month — a vacant position, or one whose person is leaving. A hire counts in headcount and cost only once Finance approves it, and approved lines are read-only until reopened. Leavers, transfers and naming the person hired are done on the Position roster.

Hiring plan

Every empty position and every position whose person is leaving, and when it is planned to be filled.

How it works

Type a hire month. Finance approves it; only approved hires count in headcount and cost, and only while nobody is assigned. A hire can't start in a month someone already sits in the position, or on a closed position.

Who can change

HR and Finance (WFP HR / WFP Finance); everyone can read.
