People

People

Everyone holding a Position in the chosen cost centre that month, and everything that can happen to them. Pick a person to act.

Actions

Hire someone… puts a person (or a new person) in a Position from a month — into an open Position, replacing whoever is there (they leave the month before), or as a handover where the leaver stays to a month you give and the two overlap. Change pay / FTE… a pay rise, promotion pay or going part-time, from a month — the months before keep their pay. Leaves… records a leaver and can plan a backfill that waits for Finance. Transfer… moves the person to an open Position in the same company; their pay and year-to-date tax go with them. Away… records time away (parental leave, long-term sick) at a pay % — they stay in headcount. Cover… puts someone in while they are away; cover counts in headcount (shown separately) and is not a joiner or leaver.

States

In post, Leaving (their last month is in the plan), Away (with the pay %), Cover, Handover (overlapping the leaver).

Cost

Every action marks cost out of date; it is brought up to date about every 5 minutes (the line under the title says when).

Who can change

HR and Finance (WFP HR / WFP Finance); everyone can read.
