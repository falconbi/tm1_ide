Match new Positions

Managers plan new Positions before HR knows about them — a planning-created (TBH) Position. When HR opens the real Position, both would count. Link them here and the planning one stops counting; the HR Position carries the hire.

How

Pick a new HR Position at the top. The planning Positions most likely to be the same job are suggested — same company, cost centre and grade, with a hire month within 3 months of HR's start. Pick one and Match. A match is never made automatically.

The lists

New HR Positions with no plan — HR Positions starting in a plan month with no hire planned on them and no planning Position linked. Late hires — approved planning Positions whose month has passed and that aren't matched yet: match, move or cancel them.

Who can change

HR and Finance (WFP HR / WFP Finance). Match per version that should follow (Forecast, Downside); Budget keeps its plan.
