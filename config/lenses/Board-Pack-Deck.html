Notes to the P&L — FY25

1 · Revenue. Revenue of $1,400k was +4.1% favourable to budget of $1,345k. Product revenue (+4.5%) and service revenue (+7.9%) were ahead of plan; other revenue was $15k unfavourable (−17.6%), reflecting the run-off of discontinued ancillary income. Revenue is recognised at point of delivery.

2 · Cost of goods sold. COGS of $560k was $20k favourable to budget (40.0% of revenue). Materials were 2.9% favourable on procurement savings; direct labour 6.7% favourable; freight in line with plan.

3 · Operating expenses. Operating expenses of $565k were $35k unfavourable to budget, driven by sales & marketing (+7.7%) on campaign timing and research & development (+9.4%) on product investment. Depreciation was flat. EBITDA of $275k represents a 19.6% margin.

4 · Interest & tax. Interest was flat at $35k. Tax of $55k reflects an effective rate of 22.9% on profit before tax of $240k, slightly above budget on the mix of earnings.
Basis of preparation. Consolidated, FY25, USD $'000. Favourable variance is green; unfavourable red. For expense lines, spending less than budget is favourable. Figures are sample data; the pack is generated, so numbers refresh from the model.
